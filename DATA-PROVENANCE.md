# Data provenance and published terms

Inputs are retrieved directly from original version-pinned CRAN package archives by setup_sources.py; no raw observations or extracted annual inputs are mirrored in this repository.

Renext 3.1-4 declares GPL (>= 2). Its inspected Brest documentation attributes SHOM sea-level observations and predictions and IRSN processing, with no explicit dataset-specific licence exclusion. This published declaration is the licensing basis used for this processed package route. We have not independently reconstructed every historical upstream rights transfer. REFMAR's separate access policy has not been established to govern this archived IRSN-processed package. Clarification inquiries do not constitute a mandatory waiting condition or a guarantee of clearance.

Archive URLs and exact SHA256 values are recorded in setup_sources.py and evidence/setup-receipt.json. Both original package DESCRIPTION notices are retained under notices/. Annual aggregation is performed locally. Published fitted tables and diagnostic graphics report this replication's analysis; source observations and original published comparison targets are not relicensed as our property.

The historical acceptance receipt's licence_cleared=false field is preserved as recorded at that test. Successful retrieval alone is not a rights determination; this release uses the published package declarations and the scoped provenance above.
